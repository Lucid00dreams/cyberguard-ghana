CYBERGUARD PHISHING DETECTOR - OFFICIAL CHROME EXTENSION
===========================================================

Installation Instructions (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera):

1. Extract this ZIP folder to your computer (e.g. Downloads or Desktop).

2. Open your browser and navigate to the Extensions page:
   - Chrome: chrome://extensions
   - Edge: edge://extensions
   - Brave: brave://extensions

3. Enable "Developer mode" toggle in the top-right corner of the Extensions page.

4. Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top left.

5. Select the extracted "cyberguard-phishing-detector" folder.

Congratulations! CyberGuard Phishing Detector is now actively protecting your browser against malicious websites, MoMo scams, and phishing emails!
